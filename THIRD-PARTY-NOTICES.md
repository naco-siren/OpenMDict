## Machine-learning model

### all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (article-content search)

The on-device article-embedding feature (`docs/article-embeddings.md`) uses
[`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2)
by the Sentence-Transformers project (Nils Reimers et al.), licensed under
the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
The model is converted to Core ML with the scripts under `scripts/model/`
and is downloaded on demand; it is not stored in this repository. The
accompanying `vocab.txt` is the BERT uncased WordPiece vocabulary that ships
with the model under the same license.
